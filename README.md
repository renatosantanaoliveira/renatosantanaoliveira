## About Me

Staff QA Engineer and Tech Lead with **18+ years** of experience in software quality, test automation strategy, and platform engineering at scale. I specialize in **QAOps**, test architecture, and **Generative AI applied to QA** — leading quality initiatives across multiple squads and platforms (Web, API, and Mobile Native).

At Grupo Casas Bahia, I led the QAOps strategy and designed reusable automation frameworks adopted by **20+ product squads**, reducing new project setup from weeks to a few days. I also led the corporate adoption of **GitHub Copilot and Gemini** for test automation, defining guardrails, governance patterns, and templates for safe and scalable use.

I write regularly on [Medium](https://medium.com/@renato-saoli) about modern test architecture, AI-driven quality, and practical governance in automation.
